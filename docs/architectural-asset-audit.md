## Missing Architectural Asset Requirements

- Empty-room shell view.
- Completed-room view matching empty shell.
- Wall construction or cladding layer view.
- Perforated/acoustic panel macro and room-scale views.
- Video-wall surround and service-access view.
- Reflected ceiling plan.
- Ceiling section or cutaway.
- Lighting-state set from same room camera.
- Finished floor, lifted tile, underfloor service and pedestal views.
- Approved flooring-system comparison imagery, if PWS 4000 / PWS 6000 content is supplied.
- Acoustic detail and treated/untreated comparison.
- Material close-ups with approved finish names.
- Plan view, room section and operator perspective.
- Collaboration and maintenance states.
- Transparent layer assets for exploded-room memory moment.

Do not use unrelated stock interiors as replacements.
