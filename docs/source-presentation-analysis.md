## 11. Suggested Final Presentation Structure

1. **Opening: Mission-Critical Operations Never Pause**  
   Begin with operational stakes, not company history.

2. **The Control-Room Challenge**  
   Show human pressure, information load, fragmented rooms, maintenance and scale limits.

3. **OnePWS Control-Room Philosophy**  
   Introduce OnePWS as the specialist in consoles, design-build solutions and ergonomic engineering.

4. **One Environment, Engineered Under One Roof**  
   Use current design-build scope: architecture, structural, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, interior design, ergonomic consultation, acoustic and illumination, international norms, FAT/SAT/warranty/AMC.

5. **Ergonomic Study & ISO 11064 Methodology**  
   Explain task review, sightlines, console dimensions and environmental review.

6. **Console Portfolio & Intelligent Feature Layer**  
   Present console ranges and design capabilities with confirmation-required tags where deployment/specification is not verified.

7. **International Compliance & Certifications**  
   Lead with strongest certifications; keep detailed certificate explanations in appendix.

8. **Project Experience**  
   Curated proof: DFCC Ahmedabad, Chandigarh ICCC, Adani Khavda, RTGC Andhra Pradesh, ACPO/APCO Ahmedabad, ITMS Noida, Shell Brunei.

9. **Manufacturing & Quality Confidence**  
   100% in-house manufacturing, machinery, raw-material/process controls, SAP traceability and improvement program.

10. **Credibility Layer**  
   Group/OnePWS metrics, awards, exhibitions, customer wall and presence map.

11. **Customer-Specific Direction**  
   Configure industry, operator count, shift model, console direction, intelligent integration, supervisor/collaboration needs.

12. **Next Step**  
   Consultation, concept layout request, technical credentials, showroom visit or proposal link.
