## General Restrictions

- Do not add medical claims.
- Do not claim injury prevention.
- Do not claim fatigue reduction as a measured outcome without evidence.
- Do not invent ergonomic dimensions, lighting values, acoustic ratings or response-time improvements.
- Do not imply technology replaces the operator.
