## 10. Recommended new presentation storyline

The current deck leads with company facts, then quickly becomes a proof gallery. A premium interactive presentation should instead build confidence through a clearer sales narrative:

1. Start with the stakes: mission-critical spaces where operator decisions, uptime and visibility matter.
2. Introduce Pyrotech Workspace as a specialist in design-build control-room environments, backed by the wider Pyrotech Group.
3. Show the end-to-end promise: strategy, ergonomics, engineering, manufacturing, installation and support.
4. Prove scale with curated metrics: 400+ control-room/design-build solutions, 500+ customers, 25 countries/design-build reach, 209 Cr. Workspace sales, 170,000 sq. ft. factory.
5. Demonstrate vertical credibility through selected flagship projects, not a flat gallery.
6. Explain the design methodology: task analysis, sightlines, console ergonomics, lux/HVAC/acoustics, cable and access-floor planning.
7. Show products as a system: consoles, access floors, interiors, cladding, tech interiors and showroom.
8. Use awards, customer logos and partnerships as trust accelerators after the solution is understood.
9. End with an action-oriented CTA: visit showroom, request consultation, or review a relevant sector case study.
