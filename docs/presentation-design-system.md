# OnePWS Presentation Design System

Output format: PowerPoint, 16:9 widescreen.  
Brand: OnePWS Private Limited.  
Design goal: a modern, premium, architectural and technically credible control-room capability deck for consultants, architects, plant heads, operations leaders and senior management.

## Design Principles

- Use one consistent master layout family across the full deck.
- Make the presentation feel engineered, spacious and precise.
- Let strong project photographs, product images, diagrams and data carry the proof.
- Keep text concise; avoid dense paragraph slides.
- Use OnePWS red as a controlled signal, not a dominant theme.
- Avoid old Pyrotech yellow/orange branding.
- Avoid generic corporate templates, decorative gradients, gaming/cyberpunk styling and excessive icons.

## Colour Palette

| Role | Colour | Hex | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary background | White | `#FFFFFF` | Main slide background for most corporate content. |
| Primary text | Black | `#0A0A0A` | Headlines, key numbers, high-emphasis labels. |
| Deep neutral | Charcoal | `#1D1F22` | Dark section dividers, footer bars, technical diagrams. |
| Mid neutral | Graphite | `#3B3F45` | Secondary headings, captions, diagram strokes. |
| Light neutral | Light grey | `#F3F4F5` | Subtle content bands, tables, image gutters. |
| Rule line | Cool grey | `#D9DDE2` | Dividers, table rules, chart axes. |
| Muted text | Grey | `#72777F` | Supporting copy, source notes, labels. |
| Brand accent | OnePWS red | `#D71920` | Section marker, key highlight, selected chart value, small callout. |
| Red tint | Pale red | `#FBE8EA` | Light emphasis background only when needed. |

Red usage limit: no more than 5-8% of any slide. Prefer red for one deliberate action, marker or proof highlight.

## Typography Hierarchy

Use a clean sans-serif family available in PowerPoint. Preferred:

- **Aptos** for widest compatibility.
- **Aptos Display** for large titles where available.
- Alternate: **Arial** only if compatibility requires it.

| Style | Size | Weight | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Section divider title | 44-52 pt | Semibold | Section opener slides. |
| Main slide title | 34-42 pt | Semibold | Standard slide title. |
| Project/product title | 30-36 pt | Semibold | Project and product feature slides. |
| Key metric | 42-56 pt | Semibold | Large proof numbers. |
| Subtitle/support line | 18-22 pt | Regular | One-line slide framing. |
| Body text | 15-18 pt | Regular | Short bullets or descriptions. |
| Caption/source | 9-11 pt | Regular | Source notes, legal and image captions. |
| Footer label | 8-9 pt | Regular/medium | Confidentiality, page number, section name. |

Rules:

- Avoid text below 9 pt except unavoidable legal content.
- Keep body text to 3-5 short bullets or 35-55 words per slide.
- Do not use condensed fonts.
- Use sentence case for most titles. Avoid all-caps except small labels.

## Slide Margins

16:9 canvas assumed: 13.333 x 7.5 in.

Standard safe area:

- Left margin: 0.65 in
- Right margin: 0.65 in
- Top margin: 0.48 in
- Bottom margin: 0.48 in

Content grid:

- 12-column invisible grid.
- Column gutter: 0.12-0.16 in.
- Primary content width: 11.9-12.0 in.
- Keep all key text inside safe area.

Do not place critical content behind footers, logos or page numbers.

## Title Placement

Use one title system throughout:

- Title top-left, aligned to the main content grid.
- Small section label above title where useful.
- Title width usually 45-60% of slide.
- For image-led project slides, title may sit in a white or charcoal side panel, never over a busy image unless the image has a quiet area.

Recommended standard title block:

- Section label: 9 pt, uppercase, letter spacing modest, OnePWS red or grey.
- Title: 36 pt, black.
- Support line: 18 pt, grey, max two lines.

## Footer System

Use a restrained technical footer on every slide except full-bleed section dividers and cover.

Footer elements:

- Left: OnePWS logo or small wordmark.
- Centre-left: section name or short deck title.
- Right: confidentiality label and page number.

Footer height:

- 0.28-0.34 in.

Footer style:

- Hairline top rule in `#D9DDE2`.
- Text in `#72777F`.
- Red only as a tiny section indicator line or dot.

## Page Numbering

Format:

- `01 / 65`, `02 / 65`, etc.

Placement:

- Bottom-right, aligned with footer.

Rules:

- No page number on cover.
- Section divider may use page number only if legally required.
- Appendix pages may use `A1`, `A2` if created outside the 65-slide core deck.

## Confidentiality Label

Preferred label:

- `CONFIDENTIAL`

Placement:

- Bottom-right footer, before page number.

Style:

- 8-9 pt uppercase, grey.
- Do not use large red confidential stamps.

Important:

- Update all confidentiality wording to OnePWS Private Limited.
- Do not reuse old Pyrotech confidentiality text unchanged.

## Master Layout Family

Use one family with controlled variants:

1. **Cover Layout**  
   Full-slide hero image or quiet technical background, OnePWS logo, title, date/version.

2. **Section Divider Layout**  
   Charcoal or white background, large section number, section title, one-line section promise.

3. **Standard Content Layout**  
   Title block top-left, content below in one or two columns.

4. **Image + Insight Layout**  
   One dominant image occupying 55-65% of slide; insight panel occupying remaining space.

5. **Data Proof Layout**  
   Large metric or chart with one explanatory statement.

6. **Project Proof Layout**  
   One large project image, short project facts, source note, confirmation-required placeholders where needed.

7. **Technical Appendix Layout**  
   Clear table or matrix, restrained typography, no decorative treatment.

Avoid creating many special-purpose masters. The deck should feel like one coherent system.

## Image Treatment

Preferred:

- One dominant image per slide.
- High-resolution project or product images.
- Architectural cropping with clear sightlines and room geometry.
- White or charcoal image frame with 1 px grey rule.

Avoid:

- Multiple unrelated images on one slide.
- Busy collages unless showing project portfolio overview.
- Heavy shadows, rounded photo cards, decorative overlays.
- Darkening images so much that project detail is lost.

Image styles:

- Full bleed only for cover, section dividers and selected project hero slides.
- Standard image frame: rectangular, square corners, no rounded card look.
- Captions: bottom-left of image frame, 9-10 pt grey.

## Data Visualisation Style

Use clear corporate charts:

- Bar charts for turnover and year-by-year growth.
- Large metric tiles for proof points.
- Simple maps for country/customer presence.
- Matrix tables for certifications and raw materials.

Chart rules:

- Black/charcoal for normal data.
- OnePWS red for the latest/current value or selected highlight.
- Light grey axes and gridlines.
- No 3D charts.
- No decorative gradients.
- No more than one major chart per slide.
- Every chart should include source page or internal confirmation note where needed.

## Project-Page Style

Project pages should feel like architectural case-study proof, not photo dumps.

Standard project layout:

- Left or top: project title and sector/location.
- Main area: one strong project photograph.
- Side panel: 3-5 facts only.
- Bottom: source note and confirmation-required fields.

Recommended fields:

- Project name
- Location
- Sector
- OnePWS scope
- Key delivered systems
- Proof point or available note
- Information unavailable where the source does not provide details

Use project spreads for paired projects only when space remains readable.

## Product-Page Style

Product slides should be precise and visual.

Standard product layout:

- Product/feature name
- One-line value proposition
- One dominant product image, render or technical diagram
- 3 short benefit points
- Confirmation-required note for unverified specs

Product visual rules:

- Prefer clean product photography or line/cutaway diagrams.
- Use red only to highlight the active feature.
- Avoid icon-heavy feature grids.
- Do not show every product range on every product slide.

## Certification-Page Style

Certification slides should communicate trust without becoming unreadable.

Recommended structure:

- One overview slide with certification categories.
- One detailed slide for safety, sustainability and ergonomics.
- Deeper certificate descriptions moved to appendix/backup.

Visual style:

- Certification badges/logos in a disciplined matrix.
- Short label and one-line meaning per certification.
- Grey table rules.
- Red highlight only for the most important proof point.

Required caution:

- Include “scope confirmation required” where certificate coverage is not fully verified.
- Do not imply all certifications apply to every product unless confirmed.

## Section-Divider Style

Section dividers should be calm and premium.

Layout:

- Mostly white or charcoal.
- Small OnePWS red line or block.
- Large section title.
- One sentence explaining what the section proves.

Avoid:

- Stock gradients.
- Big icon illustrations.
- Multiple images.
- Repeating the same divider without section-specific purpose.

## Closing-Slide Style

Closing should feel like the beginning of a customer engagement, not a generic thank-you.

Core message:

- `The Future of Control Rooms Starts Here.`

Content:

- OnePWS contact details.
- Website: `www.onepws.com`.
- Phone: `1800 889 6997`.
- Udaipur address from current deck.
- QR placeholder for consultation/proposal link.
- Updated confidentiality note.

Visual:

- Strong, calm control-room or showroom image.
- White or charcoal content panel.
- No generic “Thank You” as the primary message.

## Do Not Use

- Old Pyrotech yellow/orange as a brand accent.
- Heavy red backgrounds.
- Decorative gradients.
- Cyberpunk neon effects.
- Generic rounded cards everywhere.
- Dense bullet blocks.
- Tiny customer-logo walls in the main deck.
- Repeated section dividers with no new information.
- Multiple unrelated photos on one slide.

## Practical PowerPoint Build Notes

- Build in 16:9 widescreen.
- Create master layouts before designing individual slides.
- Lock footer and page-number positions in master slides.
- Use image placeholders with consistent crop ratios.
- Use editable PowerPoint charts where data may change.
- Keep all source/confirmation notes in speaker notes where they are not meant for the customer-facing slide.
- Use appendix/backup pages for dense tables and legal detail.
